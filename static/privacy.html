Privacy notice

This notice explains how the hotel processes personal data in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. The hotel should review this text with its legal adviser and add its registered company details.

What we collect

Name, contact details, nationality, identity or passport number, date of birth, stay details, requests, orders, payments and reviews.

Why we collect it

  • To provide your booking and stay (contract).
  • To register guests with the authorities and issue tax invoices (legal obligation).
  • To keep the hotel secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interest).
  • To send offers, only if you opted in (consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

How long we keep it

Booking data is kept while your account is active. Invoices and accounting records are kept for the period required by Egyptian tax law. When you delete your account, your personal identifiers are erased and financial records are kept in anonymous form.

Your rights

You can see, download, correct or erase your data, and withdraw consent. Sign in to My account → Privacy & data, or contact the front desk.

Security

  • Access to guest data is limited by staff role.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • All access is recorded in an audit log.
  • Backups are protected.
